How Nyxra Handles Costs & Terms

We believe in 100% transparency. There are no hidden fees, locked accounts, or proprietary traps.

  • check Client-Owned Accounts: Domains, hosting, and CRM tools are registered in your name wherever practical.
  • check Third-Party Costs: Domain fees (~₹800/yr), hosting, and any paid tool APIs are billed directly to your account.
  • check Written Proposal: Every deliverable, milestone, and timeline is confirmed in writing before payments.

Can I start with Digital Launch and upgrade later?

Yes. Many businesses launch their website first and then layer on CRM lead tracking, appointment workflows, and AI content systems as enquiry volume grows.

How many revision rounds are included?

The Digital Launch package includes two structured revision rounds. Custom scopes can define additional revision milestones in the proposal.

Do you guarantee specific sales or revenue?

No. We engineer systems that maximize clarity, lead capture, and prompt follow-up, but final sales depend on your product, market demand, and business execution.
